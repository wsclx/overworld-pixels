## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- First release: redraws Material Design icons and the weather icons of the weather forecast card as pixel art at runtime, with one art pixel size everywhere.
- Embeds the pixel font Silkscreen (SIL Open Font License 1.1) as `Overworld Pixel`.
- Only active while the theme sets `overworld-pixels: "on"`; switching themes restores the original icons.
